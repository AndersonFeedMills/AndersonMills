# Anderson Mills Website

A warm, heritage-inspired website for **Anderson Mills**, a customer project built around the identity of a rural feed store and community business.

## Project direction

The visual style is based on the supplied vintage cap design:

- Tan and cream background tones
- Deep red branding and accents
- Navy text and details
- Golden yellow sun imagery
- Farm, barn, silo, and rolling-field motifs
- Nostalgic Americana and hand-painted sign styling

The brand phrase shown in the reference image is:

> THE FEED STORE & MORE

Supporting phrase:

> MORE THAN JUST A FEED STORE

## Planned pages

- **Home** — storefront hero image, introduction, and primary calls to action
- **About** — verified business and family history
- **Products & Services** — customer-approved offerings
- **Community** — local involvement and memories
- **Photo Archive** — historic photos, signs, caps, buildings, and documents
- **Contact** — address, hours, phone, email, map, and contact form

## Important content rule

Do not invent company history, products, locations, dates, services, or customer testimonials. All public-facing facts should be confirmed by the Anderson Mills customer before launch.

## Current homepage concept

The homepage uses:

1. A wide storefront illustration as the main portal image
2. The exact business name: **Anderson Mills**
3. Clear links to the story, store information, archive, and contact page
4. A friendly, trustworthy tone suitable for a local customer-facing business

## Customer information needed

Before final development, collect:

- Official business name and capitalization
- Logo and brand files
